Helix is a desktop environment where AI agents work on your research. Each research project gets its
own agents, terminals, notebooks and files, so you can run an analysis, search the literature and
debug a pipeline at the same time, and keep every step on record.

## Who it is for

- **Biologists and biotechnologists** who want an assistant that reads their tables, runs the
  analysis and explains the result.
- **Bioinformaticians** who work with Nextflow, Snakemake, conda environments and HPC clusters.
- **Research groups** that need analyses others can audit and repeat.

## What Helix is not

- **Not a model.** Helix runs agents you already use: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode,
  Goose, Aider or any other CLI agent.
- **Not a hosted service.** Your data stays on your machine or on servers you control. Remote
  compute uses your own SSH hosts and clusters.
- **Not a black box.** Agents run real commands in real terminals, and you can open any of them.
